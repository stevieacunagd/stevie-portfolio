The Designer

Design is problem-solving with a point of view.

I’m Stevie Acuña, a graphic designer based in Santa Barbara, CA. I specialize in print, digital, and logo design — bringing a bold, considered approach to every project.

I care about work that communicates clearly and looks unmistakably good. Whether it’s a complete publication refresh or a one-page flyer, the details matter.
